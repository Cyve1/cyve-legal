Cyve Support

Cyve keeps your museum visit archive on this device. Use the tips below if something does not work as expected.

  • If Camera, Microphone, Speech Recognition, or Photos is denied, open iOS Settings, find Cyve, and enable the permission you need.
  • If a purchase does not add Visit Credits, wait for the App Store sheet to finish, then reopen the shop. Consumable credits are not restored after Clear All Data or app deletion.
  • Clear All Data permanently removes visits, gallery pages, drafts, and remaining credits on this installation.
