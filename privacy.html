Cyve Privacy Policy

Cyve is a personal museum visit archive application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Cyve on your iPhone or iPad.

What Cyve Is For

Cyve helps you log museum and gallery visits, attach photos of rooms or artworks, write or dictate notes, preview gallery pages, and spend Visit Credits to save finished pages on your device. Cyve does not provide cloud accounts, social feeds, or cross-device sync in this version.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Visit records you type or dictate, including museum name, exhibition title, artist, artwork title, year, medium, dimensions, visit date, gallery room, personal notes, route, and practice score.
  • Photos you capture with the camera or select from your photo library for a visit entry, plus rendered gallery page images saved in the app sandbox.
  • Drafts of unfinished visit entries so you can resume later on the same installation.
  • Visit Credit balance and local purchase idempotency keys related to Apple In-App Purchases.
  • Preference flags such as whether you completed the welcome screen.

Permissions We Request

  • Camera — only when you tap Take Photo to capture gallery or artwork images.
  • Photo Library — only when you choose an existing photo or save a finished gallery page to Photos.
  • Microphone — only when you start Speak Notes dictation.
  • Speech Recognition — only to convert spoken notes into text that you confirm before inserting.

Cyve does not use App Tracking Transparency, does not collect IDFA for advertising inside the product experience, and does not set a notification center delegate for push delivery on this A-side package.

Network and Third Parties

Visit content remains on your device unless you export an image to the system Photos app or complete a purchase through Apple. In-App Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple Media Services terms. Legal pages may load from GitHub Pages when opened inside the app.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete individual gallery pages or use Clear All Data in Profile to erase visits, pages, drafts, local purchase keys, and remaining credits on this device. Uninstalling the app also removes the app sandbox. Consumable Visit Credits are device-local and are not restored after deletion.

Children

Cyve is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You can deny camera, microphone, speech, or photos access in iOS Settings. Core browsing of existing local records still works; only the denied feature is unavailable. You can also stop using the app and clear local data at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions about Cyve, open the Support page linked from the app Profile screen.
